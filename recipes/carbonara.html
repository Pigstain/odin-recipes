Carbonara

Home

This is a real spaghetti carbonara recipe made the traditional Italian way, without a single drop of cream. It relies solely on egg and cheese to make a luscious, creamy carbonara sauce. Food fit for a king (or queen!) that proves simple can be magnificent.

Ingredients

  • Guanciale
  • Parmigiano reggiano
  • Egg yolks and whole eggs
  • Spaghetti
  • Pepper
  • Garlic

Steps

  1. Batons – Cut the guanciale into thick batons
  2. Finely grate the parmigiana reggiano or pecorino.
  3. Sauce – Whisk together the egg, cheese and pepper in a large bowl.
  4. Cook pasta – Bring 4 litres (4 quarts) of water to the boil with 1 tablespoon of salt.
  5. Reserve pasta cooking water – Just before draining, scoop out one cup of pasta cooking water.
  6. Cook guanciale until golden while the pasta is cooking.
  7. Toss pasta in guanciale
  8. Transfer into sauce bowl
  9. Add 1/2 cup pasta cooking water into the bowl.
  10. Mix vigorously with the handle of a wooden spoon
