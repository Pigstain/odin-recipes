Pancetta is a salt-cured pork belly meat product in a category known as salume. In Italy, it is often used to add depth to soups and pasta.

  • mixture of salt,
  • dextrose
  • spices and spice extracts,
  • odium erythorbate
  • garlic
  • sugar
  • sodium nitrate
  1. pork is salted and held in a tub of brine for 10–14 days
  2. After salting and brining, the pork is rolled, with layers of fat on the outside surrounding a meaty core.
  3. Following rolling and packing, the pork undergoes enzymatic reactions
  4. Finally, the smoked pork is held at 12–14 °C and 72–75% relative humidity for 3–4 weeks for drying
